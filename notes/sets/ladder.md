# Ladder — session guide

An ordered account of the set: ambient into dance, then back to the grid.
`notes/ontology/` holds tags; this guide holds sequence. The old dam deck
recorded only membership, so it has been retired.

## Rungs

1. **filigree** — A-root modulation ladder; `score long-form modal-shift`.
   Opened ambient and gave the night its first A anchor.
2. **klang** — F minor detour; `bed long-form loop`. Its §4 landing held the
   full texture before the move to A.
3. **seba** — A major, 116 BPM; `groove loop`. Parallel A shift, seats 1–8;
   no overflow from klang.
4. **venlafaxina** — A minor, 124 BPM; `groove loop modal-shift`. Lifted a
   minor third in §8, then §11a/§11b faded the lifted band and resolved home.
5. **kumpania** — A harmonic minor, 104 BPM; `long-form modal-shift pulse`.
   Entered on venlafaxina's overflow exit; `clutchIn` made the 7/8 grid cut
   deliberately sharp. §6 lifted a fourth to D harmonic minor; seat 6's
   guiro is the **scrape** (`notes/learning/glossary.md`).
6. **strobe** — E minor, 140 BPM; `groove long-form modal-shift`. D → E whole
   tone up. Doors landing let the break arrive; §§8–11 lifted a tone to
   F♯ minor (`key:E` + `key:F#`).
7. **grin** — E Dorian, 120 BPM; `groove loop`. E minor → Dorian (C♮ → C♯);
   `swing16` kept the pocket human so §4's straight grid had something to
   push against.
8. **mezzanine** — G minor, 130 BPM; `groove loop`. E → G minor third up;
   exact spine match, with `swing16` hats and rim carrying the pocket forward.
9. **daywork** — G mixolydian, 96 BPM; `groove long-form score`. Same G root,
   minor brightening to mixolydian; the 2-step loop trades for a written
   long-form arc back into ambient.

## What carried it

- Root continuity: A across filigree → seba → venlafaxina → kumpania, with
  klang as the F-minor detour; E across strobe → grin; G across mezzanine →
  daywork.
- Spines moved: groove/loop → long-form/pulse → groove/long-form → groove loop
  → groove long-form/score. `modal-shift` carried the formal lifts.
- Landings and overflow exits did the graceful work; tempo and key were
  secondary.
- The ambient-to-dance bridge was legible because venlafaxina's exit and
  kumpania's grid cut were both audible, not smoothed away.
- Swing-to-grid contrast in grin was the drama; no extra transposition needed.
