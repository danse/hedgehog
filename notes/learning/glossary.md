# Glossary — track-role words

Headers and comments use short, evocative names for recurring voices and
states. This is the central place for what those words mean; exact seats,
samples and parameter values stay in the tracks. For Tidal mechanics see
`gotchas.md`; for tag terms see `../ontology/README.md`.

- **scrape** — the guiro/noise percussion role: `kumpania`'s `d6`, built from
  `casio:2*7`, bright-filtered, short decay, accented on the 7/8 groups.
  `campina` uses the same seat as a **wood** tick (`tok:0`); kumpania turns
  that wood role into a scrape. In §8, “scrape doubles” means the accents get
  louder, not that a second rhythm enters.
- **wood** — the short wooden tick role (`tok:0` in `campina`), answering on
  the short groups. Kumpania's scrape inherits its seat and rhythmic function.
