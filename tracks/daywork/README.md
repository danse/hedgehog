# Daywork

Third track in the Cantilever/Abutment world. *The Works* keeps moving: a day
in the yard, physical and funny, mostly below the level of the argument. 96 BPM
home, G mixolydian; acoustic ensemble plus object Foley; seven sections in four
acts, open coda.

Eval `00-setup.tidal` once, then play in order:

`01-first-light` → `02-gate` → `03-lift` → `04-sweeper` →
`05-canteen` → `06-handoff` → `07-standby`

Global notes (orbit map, tuning, palette, levels) are at the top of
`00-setup.tidal`; story beats and performance instructions are at the top of
each section file. Handoff's pling is a live one-shot; Standby's fade and
`hush` are separate steps.
